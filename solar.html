Solahart L Series

Roof mounted, efficient and reliable

Solahart’s roof mounted tank systems save space at ground level and their elegant simplicity makes them efficient and reliable.

The Solahart 300L Series is a roof mounted, open circuit solar water heater specifically designed to provide economical service in medium to high solar gain areas.

Also available in a 181L tank with 1 panel, the smaller volume of the 181L makes it ideal where low volumes of solar hot water are required, for example small units or retirees.

Solahart LCSD Series

Higher efficiency for low to medium solar gain

The Solahart LCSD Series is a roof mounted, open circuit system designed to provide higher efficiency for use in low to medium solar gain areas.

The LCS collector’s absorber plate is coated with the Mirotherm® sputtered selective surface. The Mirotherm absorption and anti-reflection layers provide high solar absorption, and an infrared reflecting layer ensures low thermal emission, making the most of the available solar radiation.
